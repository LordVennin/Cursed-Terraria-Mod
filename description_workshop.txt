[h1]Bag of Chaos[/h1]
A Terraria mod that is not meant to be taken seriously.

[list]
[*]Fart Reverb.mp3 - 1 damage, knockback measured in screens
[*]Block Rod - pick up chunks of terrain and throw them
[*]Sprite Slasher - legs on heads, heads on legs
[*]Glue Gun - everything sticks to everything
[*]Instant / Unreasonable / Omni movement accessories
[*]NPC Lawnmower - keeps your town nice and tidy
[*]Drinkable Air, Drinkable Lava, Solid Snake
[*]Sky in a Bottle, Null Gravity Beacon, Weather Beacons
[*]Explosive Healing Kit, TM Flash, Text Boxing Gloves
[*]Pong Power, Chain Can, Rattling Gun, Pocket Rats, Tool Gun
[*]Rocket Pack and Driller Pack dashes, Bottles of Zig and Zag
[*]Muncher armor, Bouncing Jelly Storm, Night of Moon Shoes
[*]Delirium and the Corporate Zombie Invasion (featuring the Zombie CEO)
[/list]

All sprites are placeholder programmer art.
