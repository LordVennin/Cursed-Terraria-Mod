Bag of Chaos: a Terraria mod that is not meant to be taken seriously.

Weapons, tools, accessories, events and weather built from a loose pile of ideas:
Fart Reverb.mp3, the Block Rod, the Sprite Slasher, the NPC Hand, the Credit Cannon, the Glue Gun,
instant/unreasonable movement, the NPC Lawnmower, Drinkable Air (and Lava), the Alchemical Dehydrator,
Sky in a Bottle, the 3D debuff, Null Gravity Beacons, Explosive Healing Kit, TM Flash, Chain Can,
Weather Beacons, Slippery Frogs, the Gparrling Hook, Pong Power, Rocket and Driller Packs,
Bottles of Zig and Zag, the Muncher armor set, Jelly Storms, the Night of Moon Shoes, Liquid/Solid/Venom Snake,
the Tool Gun, Text Boxing Gloves, rat guns, stretchy bullets, Chaos Solution, Delirium, and the Corporate Zombie Invasion.

Every sprite is placeholder programmer art. Pull requests with real pixel art are very welcome.
